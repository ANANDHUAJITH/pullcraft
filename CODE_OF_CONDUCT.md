# Code of Conduct

PullCraft is a creative space for people of every background and skill level, including people who have never written code.

- Be kind, patient and specific. Critique builds and code, never people.
- No harassment, hate, sexual content or discrimination, in builds, titles, descriptions or discussions.
- Don't use the world to carry spam, ads or malicious links.
- Maintainers may blank a plot (commit an empty one), remove content, or block accounts that break these rules.

Report problems through GitHub's "Report content" or the repository's Security tab. Maintainers will respond privately.
